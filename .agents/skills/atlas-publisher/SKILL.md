## 2. UI & Typographic Standards (Aligned with Graphy & Guten)

1. **Swiss Modular Grid & Active Negative Space**:
   - Exposed subtle hairlines (`1px solid`), clear structural zones, generous margins, and intentional whitespace.
2. **Strict Typographic Hierarchy & Reading Measure**:
   - **Level 1 (Display/Hero)**: High-character editorial serif/display with tight tracking for chapter titles and mental model names.
   - **Level 2 (Structural Subheads & Decks)**: Clean geometric sans-serif with active verbs and value-focused decks.
   - **Level 3 (Deep Reading Body & Monospace Specs)**: Optimal 50–65 character line measure (`max-w-prose`), `1.65` line height for effortless long-form comprehension, paired with crisp monospace (`JetBrains Mono` / `Fira Code`) for formulas, metrics, and node IDs.
3. **60-30-10 Explanatory Color System**:
   - **60% Dominant Surface**: Warm editorial paper (`#FAF8F5` light / `#0B0F17` dark obsidian).
   - **30% Structural Contrast**: Deep ink slate (`#111827` / `#1E293B`) for cards, diagrams, and matrices.
   - **10% Semantic Signal Accents**: International Orange (`#EA580C`), Electric Cobalt (`#2563EB`), and Emerald (`#059669`) used strictly to signal state, category, and interactive focus.
